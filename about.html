About This Tool

How the calculator works and why it's built this way.

What it does

This calculator estimates how many calories you need each day based on your age, gender, height, weight, and activity level. It gives you three targets so you can pick the one that matches your goal:

Lose Weight Maintain Weight Gain Weight

The formula

Your daily needs are calculated in two steps. First, we estimate your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) — the calories your body burns at rest — using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, widely regarded as one of the most accurate BMR formulas available:

Men: BMR = 10 × weight(kg) + 6.25 × height(cm) − 5 × age + 5
Women: BMR = 10 × weight(kg) + 6.25 × height(cm) − 5 × age − 161

Next, we multiply your BMR by an activity factor to get your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) — the calories you burn in a typical day, including exercise and daily movement.

How the three targets are set

  • Maintain Weight — your TDEE as-is, to keep your current weight stable.
  • Lose Weight — TDEE minus 500 calories, roughly a 0.5 kg (1 lb) per week loss.
  • Gain Weight — TDEE plus 500 calories, roughly a 0.5 kg (1 lb) per week gain.

A few notes

These numbers are estimates based on population averages, not a medical prescription. Individual metabolism can vary due to genetics, muscle mass, medical conditions, and other factors. If you have specific health goals or conditions, please consult a doctor or registered dietitian before making major changes to your diet.
